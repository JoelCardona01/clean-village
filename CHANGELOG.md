# Clean Village — Changelog

## 1.0.0+mc26.3
- First release for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric, NeoForge and Forge; tested on Fabric, Forge 66.1.8 and NeoForge 26.3.0.100).
- Dirty houses in new villages (all 5 types), sad sitting owner with a story book.
- Soapy Sponge: one tool for every kind of dirt (right or left click), recharge in water.
- Progress bar, owner waits at the door, celebration with jingle, letter and rewards.
- Hold right click now scrubs grime, mold and cobwebs (previously only left click did); grime is no longer generated on already dirty blocks.
- Hero of the Village, reputation boost, Family Portrait decoration.
- English, Spanish, French, German and Italian.
